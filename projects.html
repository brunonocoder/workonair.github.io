Beauty Bot: agentic AI market intelligence

A no-code multi-agent AI on Shopee's internal SMART platform that briefs the team on Korea and China beauty markets every morning.

Problem

KAMs and team leads needed to keep up with daily movements in Korea and China beauty, but reading, filtering and summarizing news across sources took real time every morning. There was no fast way to surface the most relevant signals and turn them into next steps for Shopee sellers.

What I built

Every morning the bot scans trusted e-commerce, FMCG and Asia beauty sources, picks the 3 most recent and relevant articles, summarizes them, estimates the business impact for Shopee, and delivers structured insights with recommended actions before the team starts work.

How it works

  1. Daily trigger. Runs on an automated morning schedule.
  2. Web search agent. Scans trusted sources from the last 3 months.
  3. Analysis agent. GPT-4.1-mini and Gemini summarize and score each article.
  4. Shopee knowledge. Grounds insights in Shopee Uni and internal playbooks.
  5. Output agent. Delivers a structured report to the team.

Sources include Tech in Asia, Ecommercenews.asia, Nikkei Asia and Jing Daily. Focus markets: Korea beauty, China beauty, SEA e-commerce and FMCG trends.

Seller Portal: a centralized KAM data hub

One Google Sheet that pulls every key seller metric from Datasuite each day, so KAMs open a ready-made view instead of copy-pasting.

Problem

Seller Center scatters KPI data across many tabs. Every KAM spent 40–50 minutes each morning copy-pasting before starting real work.

How it works

  1. Datasuite sync. Auto-syncs every day at 16:00.
  2. KPI metrics sheet. GMV, orders, ads, content, AMS and FBS.
  3. FBS tracker. Stock health and SKU coverage.
  4. AI recommender. Suggests next actions and pitch scripts.
  5. KAM view. One sheet, by KAM and by shop, ready on open.

Covers revenue, marketing channels, orders, AMS, content, FBS stock health, buyer profiles, week and month comparisons, and AI-generated next steps.

ARIMA model for VCB stock price prediction

A time-series forecast of Vietcombank's share price built only from its own price history.

Problem

Stock prices are notoriously hard to predict. The challenge was to reach meaningful accuracy on VCB using only historical price data, with no external indicators.

What I built

Led a team of 5 to design, train and validate an ARIMA forecasting model in Python: data collection, stationarity testing (ADF), parameter selection (p, d, q) and evaluation. Presented at Young UEH Researchers 2024.
