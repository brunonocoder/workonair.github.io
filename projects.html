01. Undergraduate Research 2024

ARIMA Forecasting Model —
VCB Stock Price Prediction

UEH University · Young UEH Researchers 2024 · Team of 5

The Problem

Stock price prediction is notoriously difficult. The challenge: build a model using only historical price data — no external indicators — and achieve meaningful accuracy on Vietcombank (VCB) stock.

What I Built

Led a team of 5 to design, train, and validate an ARIMA time-series forecasting model on VCB historical data. Handled data collection, stationarity testing (ADF), model parameter selection (p,d,q), and evaluation in Python. Presented findings as part of Young UEH Researchers 2024.

04. Current · Shopee · AI 2025

Beauty Bot —
Agentic AI Market Intelligence

Shopee · FMCG & Beauty Category · Powered by SMART (SEA Multi-Agent Realisation Technology)

The Problem

KAMs and team leads needed to stay ahead of daily market movements in Korea and China beauty — but reading, filtering, and summarizing industry news across multiple sources took significant time every morning. There was no structured, fast way to surface the most relevant signals and translate them into actionable steps for Shopee sellers.

What I Built

Designed and deployed Beauty Bot — a no-code multi-agent AI system built on Shopee's internal SMART platform. Every morning, the bot automatically scans trusted sources across e-commerce, FMCG, and Asia beauty markets, filters the 3 most recent and relevant articles, summarizes key points, estimates business impact for Shopee, and delivers structured insights with actionable next steps — all before the team starts their day.
