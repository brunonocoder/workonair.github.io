01. Undergraduate Research 2024

ARIMA Forecasting Model —
VCB Stock Price Prediction

UEH University · Young UEH Researchers 2024 · Team of 5

The Problem

Stock price prediction is notoriously difficult. The challenge: build a model using only historical price data — no external indicators — and achieve meaningful accuracy on Vietcombank (VCB) stock.

What I Built

Led a team of 5 to design, train, and validate an ARIMA time-series forecasting model on VCB historical data. Handled data collection, stationarity testing (ADF), model parameter selection (p,d,q), and evaluation in Python. Presented findings as part of Young UEH Researchers 2024.

03. Current · Shopee 2025

Seller Portal —
Centralized KAM Data System

Shopee · Key Account Management · Beauty & FMCG Category

The Problem

Shopee's Seller Center scatters data across multiple tabs — campaign metrics, FBS status, offsite performance, and GMV all live in different places. Every KAM spent 40–50 minutes each morning manually collecting and copying data before they could even start their actual work.

What I Built

Seller Portal — a centralized Google Sheet that auto-pulls and consolidates all key seller metrics from multiple data sources into one unified, always-up-to-date view. KAMs open one sheet in the morning and immediately see the full picture: GMV, campaign participation, FBS rates, and offsite spend — all by KAM and by shop. Collaborated with team leads and the Beauty Category Manager to scope requirements and design the data architecture.
